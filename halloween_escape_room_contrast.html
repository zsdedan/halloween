🎃 Halloween Quest

Teacher Guide

A1–A2 · Ages 8–12 · Suggested time: 60–80 minutes.

Lesson route: Warm-up → Vocabulary → Reading → Monster Lab → Speaking → Five Locks → Monster Creator → Checklist.

Teaching tips: Share your screen or let learners open this file. Ask for full spoken answers. For A1, allow short sentences. For A2, ask for reasons and examples.

Answer Key

Warm-up: pumpkin, bat, candy, ghost.

Vocabulary: pumpkin—orange vegetable; ghost—friendly spirit; bat—flying animal; witch—magic person; candy—sweet food; moon—night sky object; spider—eight-legged animal; mask—face covering.

Reading: 1 B (October 31), 2 A (costumes), 3 C (Ireland), 4 B (loved ones).

Monster labels: Luna—one eye; Bobo—three eyes; Pip—two horns; Mimi—four arms.

Escape codes: Lock 1 = 7, Lock 2 = 8, Lock 3 = 5, Lock 4 = 4, Lock 5 = 2. Final: 78542.

Word scramble: MONSTER. True/False: T, F, T, F, T. Sequence: seed → sprout → flower → pumpkin.

Level 1: Give word banks and sentence starters. Level 2: Hide hints and ask for reasons with because.

Interaction: Drag with a mouse, or tap a word and then the matching box. Keyboard: focus a word, press Enter, focus a box and press Enter.
